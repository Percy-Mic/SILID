## Textures and HDRIs — Poly Haven

**CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication).** No attribution is required,
commercial use and redistribution are allowed. Sources: <https://polyhaven.com/>
· licence: <https://polyhaven.com/license>.

The runtime forest additionally redistributes the scanned foliage maps from
`island_tree_01`, `island_tree_02`, and `fern_02` under
`public/assets/textures/`. Their source URLs, authors, CC0 terms, and checksums
are recorded in the corresponding provenance files under
`Blender/AssetLibrary/Source/PolyHaven/`.

Maps are fetched by [scripts/fetch_pbr_textures.mjs](scripts/fetch_pbr_textures.mjs)
(materials) and land in `Blender/AssetLibrary/Source/PolyHaven/<slug>/`, with a
full record — source page, authors, per-map URL, md5, bytes, date — in
`<slug>.provenance.json` beside them. The surface table those maps belong to is
`Blender/AssetLibrary/Source/PolyHaven/manifest.json`: role, slug, real-world
tile size in metres, and which file is the colour, normal, roughness or ORM map.
The Blender build reads it through `silid_pbr.py`; the game reads the same table
through `src/world/pbr.js`, so the cinematic and the build cannot drift apart.

## Drawn in code — the house's pictures and the rite book

The 23 paintings in the mansion and the book on the oratory table are **not
downloaded assets**. Every canvas is painted procedurally at load time by
[src/world/Paintings.js](src/world/Paintings.js) and
[src/world/Ritual.js](src/world/Ritual.js) — the same technique the forest, the
estate and the roadside props already use — so there is no licence, no file to
lose and no texture to attribute.

The two Sketchfab items the owner supplied as references (*Paintings Wall
Decorations – low poly pack* by Karolina Renkiewicz, and *The Book of Cain* by
Kristofer Lelinde) were used as **art direction only**: composition, subject and
palette. Neither was downloaded or redistributed. Sketchfab models are
generally account-gated and not CC0, which is the standing reason this project's
authored art is drawn in code; the well above is the one Sketchfab model that is
genuinely CC-BY and is shipped, and it carries the credit above for that reason.

## Road and roadside materials (added 2026-10-27)

**CC0 1.0** — https://polyhaven.com/license. Downloaded at 1K JPG from the
Poly Haven files API, resized in the browser to the game's texture budget (the
carriageway alone is capped at 1024 px, everything else at 512 — see
[src/world/materials.js](src/world/materials.js)). Full per-map provenance, with
URL, byte count and md5, is in
`Blender/AssetLibrary/Source/PolyHaven/<slug>.provenance.json`.

The tile size column is the physical size of the photograph (the Poly Haven
`dimensions` field), and it is now stated in code: `TILE` in
`src/world/materials.js`. A surface's UVs are written in metres and the material
is scaled once by 1/tile, which is the one rule that fixes texture scale across
the whole road.

* The table at the top of this file says it is generated by
  `scripts/asset_licenses.mjs` and that maps are fetched by
  `scripts/fetch_pbr_textures.mjs`. Neither script is present in this repository

  generation pipeline is still missing, not just undocumented.
* Nothing in this project redistributes an asset whose licence forbids it. Where
  a model or texture is *not* CC0 it does not get committed unless its licence

  attribution lines have to survive in the build.
* HDRIs (`*_hdri_*.hdr`) are lighting-only: they are used to render reference
  stills in Blender and are not baked into any exported game asset.
