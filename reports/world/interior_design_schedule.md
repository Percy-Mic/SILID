# Mansion interior design schedule

Room additions use downloaded, textured Poly Haven models at 1K where available. Common finish layer: aged walnut dado panels and chair rails, worn floor/wall PBR, uneven warm practical lights, sourced sconces and bedroom mirrors.

## Household detail pass (2026-11-01)

Surface props were placed from the actual furniture anchors after the room
layouts were built. Poly Haven CC0 vase and mantel clock models dress selected
sideboards, bedroom dressers, studies, and entry consoles. The potted plant
model is placed in clear foyer and lounge corners after checking nearby
furniture and door swing zones. Sitting rooms receive a compact tea service;
dining rooms keep their larger tea service. Kitchens receive a small aged
enamel kettle beside the existing prep board and produce bowl. Existing wine
bottles, barrels, books, candles, cutlery, bathroom fixtures, and sconces remain
as room-specific details. The final master packs the referenced maps.

## Roof and stairs

- Six grand stair flights and a basement flight. Grand stair tread faces are softened; nosings carry fitted runners; stair rails have turned collars and corrected landing rail segments; a sourced chandelier hangs in the stairwell.
- Weathered slate covers all seven enclosed fourth-floor rooms and three cross-gables. The outer azotea remains intentionally walkable and uncovered, preserving the service hatch and ladder route.
- **Clear circulation:** B-06 Service Passage and the FF2-02 / FF3-02 South Walkways remain free of floor furniture. Their 2 m widths keep the route open; wall-mounted sconces and wainscoting provide detail.

## Review limits

The saved master passed 29 Blender structural checks, including zero floating or
